Filipino Chicken Curry Recipe

Final Product

Background

Chicken Curry is a flavorful and comforting Filipino dish made with tender chicken cooked in a rich and creamy coconut milk sauce, combined with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and aromatic spices. It has a delicious combination of savory and mildly spicy flavors that makes every bite satisfying. This popular Filipino dish is perfect for anyone who enjoys a warm, creamy, and delicious homemade meal.

Chicken Curry is served to students, employees, families, and anyone looking for a delicious and satisfying meal. It is perfect for people who enjoy flavorful Filipino dishes and want a hearty meal for lunch or dinner.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15–20 minutes 35–45 minutes 4–5 people Moderate

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 500g chicken (cut into serving pieces)
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 medium carrot, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup water or chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

For the Seasoning:

  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Optional Toppings:

  • Chopped spring onions
  • Fresh chili peppers
  • Extra bell peppers
  • Fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Wash and clean the chicken thoroughly, then cut it into serving pieces if needed.
  2. Peel and cut the potatoes and carrots into cubes or slices.
  3. Chop the onion, mince the garlic, and slice the bell peppers.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  6. Add the curry powder and fish sauce, then stir until the chicken is well coated.
  7. Pour in the water or chicken broth and let it simmer until the chicken is tender.
  8. Add the potatoes and carrots, then cook until they become tender.
  9. Pour in the coconut milk and stir gently until the sauce becomes creamy.
  10. Add the bell peppers and season with salt and ground black pepper according to your taste.
  11. Let the chicken curry simmer until the sauce thickens and all the ingredients are fully cooked.
  12. Transfer to a serving dish and garnish with optional toppings.
  13. Serve hot with steamed rice and enjoy your delicious Filipino Chicken Curry!
