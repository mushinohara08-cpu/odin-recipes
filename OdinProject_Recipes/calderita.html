Filipino Chicken Caldereta Recipe

Final Product

Background

Chicken Caldereta is a delicious and flavorful Filipino dish made with tender chicken simmered in a rich tomato-based sauce, combined with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and other savory ingredients. It has a delicious balance of sweet, savory, and slightly tangy flavors that makes every bite satisfying. This comforting Filipino dish is perfect for anyone who enjoys a hearty and flavorful homemade meal.

Chicken Caldereta is served to students, employees, families, and anyone looking for an affordable, delicious, and filling meal. It is perfect for people who enjoy traditional Filipino dishes and want a satisfying meal for lunch or dinner.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15–20 minutes 40–50 minutes 4–5 people Moderate

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 500g chicken (cut into serving pieces)
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 medium carrot, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 cup chicken broth or water
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

For the Seasoning:

  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce (optional)
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Optional Toppings:

  • Green olives
  • Chopped spring onions
  • Extra grated cheese
  • Fresh chili peppers

Steps

  1. Wash and clean the chicken thoroughly, then cut it into serving pieces if needed.
  2. Peel and cut the potatoes and carrots into cubes or slices.
  3. Chop the onion, mince the garlic, and slice the bell peppers.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  6. Add soy sauce, paprika, salt, and ground black pepper, then stir well.
  7. Pour in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, and chicken broth. Mix thoroughly.
  8. Cover the pan and let the chicken simmer until tender and fully cooked.
  9. Add the potatoes and carrots, then cook until they become tender.
  10. Add the bell peppers and green olives, if desired.
  11. Stir in the grated cheese and let the sauce simmer until thick and flavorful.
  12. Adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper according to your taste.
  13. Transfer to a serving dish and serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy your delicious Chicken Caldereta!
