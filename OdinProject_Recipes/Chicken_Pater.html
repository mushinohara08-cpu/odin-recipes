Filipino Chicken Pater Recipe

Final Product

Background

Chicken Pater is a flavorful and satisfying Filipino delicacy made with tender, juicy chicken seasoned with delicious spices and served over perfectly cooked rice. Wrapped in fragrant banana leaves, it offers a rich aroma and a mouthwatering taste in every bite. Affordable, filling, and packed with flavor, Chicken Pater is the perfect meal for anyone craving a delicious and satisfying Filipino dish.

Chicken Pater is served to students, employees, families, and anyone looking for an affordable, delicious, and filling meal. It is perfect for people who enjoy flavorful Filipino dishes and want a convenient meal for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings Difficulty
15–20 minutes 30–40 minutes 4–5 people Moderate

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 500g chicken (boneless or chicken breast)
  • 4 cups cooked rice
  • 4–5 pieces banana leaves
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Salt to taste

For the Chicken Sauce

  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ cup chicken broth

Optional Toppings

  • Fried garlic
  • Chopped spring onions
  • Boiled egg
  • Chili sauce

Steps

  1. Wash and clean the chicken thoroughly.
  2. Mince the garlic and chop the onion.
  3. Clean the banana leaves and briefly heat them to make them flexible.
  4. Cook the rice until soft and fluffy, then set aside.
  5. Heat cooking oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  6. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  7. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, and ground black pepper.
  8. Pour in the chicken broth and let it simmer until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  9. Add sugar and adjust the seasonings according to your taste.
  10. Place a portion of cooked rice in the center of the banana leaf.
  11. Add the cooked chicken on top of the rice and pour some sauce over it.
  12. Fold the banana leaves neatly to wrap the rice and chicken.
  13. Serve while warm and enjoy your delicious Chicken Pater!
